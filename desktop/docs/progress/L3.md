## Known risks / bugs

- Background shows `0 fps` in the status bar under xvfb and is hidden behind the opaque dock; whether a dynamic
  background is visible anywhere in the default layout is not verified (the glass recipe keeps chrome opaque).
- A terminal panel unmounted without `beforeClose` (e.g. a layout reset) leaves its pty running.
- `splitActive` copies a terminal's `ptyId`; the terminal panel detects this and spawns a new pty.
- The last New-session spec is stored in `appState.panels['session.last']` (no dedicated AppState slot).
- Ticket panel: Approve is hidden unless its gate is ok; only one of Archive/Unarchive shows (server `archived` flag).

- Whether the embedder CSP in `index.html` affects the `<webview>` guest is unverified.
